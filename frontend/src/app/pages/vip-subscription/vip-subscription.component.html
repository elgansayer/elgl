Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between plans?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately. If you downgrade, you'll retain premium features until the end of your billing period.
How does location spoofing work?
VIP users can set a mock location in their profile settings. This overrides your real GPS coordinates in discovery searches, allowing you to connect with speakers from specific cities or regions worldwide. Your real location remains private.
What's included in the Developer Tier?
The Developer Tier includes everything from Consumer VIP plus API key generation, 600 requests per minute rate limit, webhook integration, diagnostic logging, developer analytics dashboard, and priority developer support. Perfect for building custom integrations.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. No long-term contracts or hidden fees. Your premium features remain active until the end of your billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through Stripe. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.
