Single combined "Social" hub representing the social feature area rather than a screen per route. The header bell badge and moments compose bar/post card mirror moments-feed.component.html's top app bar and feed article (like/comment/correct/ translate action row, target-language chip). The events section mirrors events-feed.component.ts's upcoming/past filter tabs and event card, plus a condensed month grid standing in for events-calendar.component.ts's full calendar (today ringed in primary, event-bearing days dotted). The groups section mirrors groups-discovery.component.ts's topic pills and join/joined group cards, kept in the accent token as in the real component (a distinct token from primary, used nowhere else on this page). The communities section mirrors communities.component.html's create form and community grid/groups list - that source file still hardcodes bg-indigo-600/border-indigo-500/ text-white, retinted here to primary/on-fill per the rest of the design system rather than copied verbatim. The favourites section mirrors favourites.component.html's tab chips and item cards with voice/moment badge chips and the warning-toned notes callout. The notification preferences card mirrors notification-preferences.component.ts's per-category push/badge checkboxes, do-not-disturb toggle, and quiet-hours time inputs. The notifications inbox rows mirror notifications-inbox.component.html's unread-tinted row treatment with primary dot indicator versus read rows. Omitted: full comment threads and ghost-correction/voice-recorder modals on moments, the community creation flow's validation states, and the notification centre's tab filters and loading/empty states.
