Single combined "More" hub representing the scattered legal/support/misc feature area rather than a screen per route. The stats card mirrors my-stats.component.ts's summary tiles (messages in secondary/Tide, corrections in success, moments in warning) below a simplified 7-day study-hours bar in lieu of the real chart.js line/pie charts. The quest cards mirror quests.component.ts's in-progress vs completed states (accent progress fill, success badge on completion). The lesson card mirrors lessons.component.ts's accent-bordered row with proficiency badge and completed checkmark. The practice row stands in for ai-conversation.component.ts. The FAQ item mirrors help-centre.component.ts's question/answer card, alongside nav shortcuts to the help centre and help-about routes. The Help & About preview records the build-generated app version, CI run plus commit build number, and production dependency licence manifest contract. The legal block is a representative excerpt in the same typographic style as pages/legal/terms.component.ts and privacy.component.ts (both already clean of hardcoded colour, so no sample needed beyond typography), followed by nav rows to privacy/GDPR (components/gdpr), change password, device transfer, and account deletion (components/account-deletion) with its delete action in the danger token. Omitted: full chart rendering, live quest/lesson lists, and the individual destination screens themselves (each reached via its nav row, out of scope for this preview).
