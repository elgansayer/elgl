Audio room mirrors audio-room.component.html's host card, 2-column speaker stage grid (host/co-host badges, speaking/muted state, optional video-stream indicator) and 4-column listener audience grid - participant counts trimmed to a representative sample instead of the full room roster. The in-call card mirrors video-call.component.ts's full-screen dialog: local PiP preview, call-duration timer, screen-share badge and the mute/camera/share/PiP/end-call toolbar. Per its source (bg-surface-900 hardcoded, not theme-branched by the component's own design intent), this card is rendered with fixed literal dark colours rather than the --surface-900 token, so it deliberately does not respond to the light/dark toggle above - the only intentional off-token colours in this file. Omitted: the connecting and error states, the live-chat overlay, and the "remote screen share + camera thumbnail" dual-video layout - all conditional states, not the steady-state screen.
