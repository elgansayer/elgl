# 📋 Consolidated Product Backlog

*Organized by complete user outcomes rather than individual technical chores.*

## 💬 1-on-1 Chat & Messaging
- Advanced Messaging Experience (Edit messages, disappearing messages, reply threads, reactions, read/delivery receipts).
- Search & Organize (Search within chats, star/favorite messages, archive and pin conversations).
- Media & Voice Sharing (Hold-to-record voice notes, short video notes, doodle sharing).
- AI & Translation Assistance (AI conversation starters, pre-send grammar checker, message translation).
- Group Conversations (Topic-based group chats, group-level text correction tools).
- Safety & Privacy (Message forwarding labels, content moderation, end-to-end encryption for personal messages/calls).

## 📰 Moments & Social Feed
- Personalized Discovery (Algorithmic "For You" feed, daily recommended partners).
- Rich Media Sharing (Multi-image lightboxes, voice intros, quick polls).
- Community Interaction (Like/comment notifications, @mentions, "Liked By" lists).
- Event & Group Discovery (Centralized events feed, find scheduled audio rooms and study groups).
- Engagement Tracking (My Stats dashboard, correction quality ratings, user milestone badges).

## 👤 User Profiles & Settings
- Comprehensive Profile Identity (Proficiency levels, custom tags/interests, audio introductions, linked social accounts).
- Profile Customization (Cover photos, layout appearance settings).
- Privacy & Accessibility Control ("Who Viewed Me" logs, visibility toggles, dynamic font scaling, data deletion workflows).
- Account Management (Do Not Disturb scheduling, block/report management).

## 🎙️ Live Audio Rooms & Video
- Live Broadcasting (Host controls, stage grid management, audience participation, mute/kick actions).
- Interactive Live Features (Soundboards, virtual gift animations, animated audio equalizers).
- Scheduled Events (Language Parties, RSVP/attendance tracking).
- Advanced Meeting Tools (In-room chat overlay, shared notes panel, split-screen layouts).

## 🔍 Discovery & Search
- Location-Based Matchmaking (GPS-based "Nearby" search, interactive maps, VIP location spoofing).
- Serious Learner Matching (Algorithms favoring users with active streaks, high correction ratios, mutual interests).
- Global Filtering (Search by native/target language, proficiency level, age, voice room activity).

## 🧠 Learning, Vocabulary & AI Tools
- Vocabulary Building (Flashcard creation, Spaced Repetition (SRS) scheduling, partial credit scoring for typos).
- Real-Time Definitions (Click-to-translate, dictionary definitions, pronunciation audio).
- AI Explanations (Context-aware grammar breakdown, "Simplify this text").
- Structured Learning (Dedicated verb conjugation trainers, IPA alphabet modules, multiple-choice quizzes).
- Gamified Learning (Daily quests, learning goals, streak tracking).

## 👑 Monetization & VIP
- Premium Membership (Unlimited AI tools, location spoofing, incognito browsing, custom app icons/themes).
- Virtual Economy (Virtual coin wallet, daily login rewards).
- In-App Purchases (Restore purchases workflow, gift packs, animated stickers).
- Monetization Anti-Cheat (App Store receipt validation for coin balances).

## ✨ Technical Foundation & System Health
- Real-Time Infrastructure (WebSocket typing indicators, connection rate limits).
- Platform Consistency (Offline caching/IndexedDB, Service Workers, Angular Universal SSR).
- Compliance & Data Privacy (Automated PII scrubbing, COPPA age-gating, DMCA takedown flow, complete Terms/Privacy Policy integration).
- Performance & Media Processing (Client-side image compression, audio compression, on-the-fly video transcoding).
- Application Stability (Diagnostic quizzes for new sign-ups, global error handling/crash reporting).
